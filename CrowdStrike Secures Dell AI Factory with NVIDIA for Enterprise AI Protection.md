Cybersecurity leader CrowdStrike has announced a strategic security integration with Dell Technologies and NVIDIA to protect enterprise artificial intelligence deployments. Bringing the CrowdStrike Dell [AI Factory](https://soc-news.com/crowdstrike-secures-dell-ai-factory-with-nvidia/) solution to market enables organizations to deploy high-performance generative AI infrastructure, large language models (LLMs), and autonomous agent workflows with embedded, AI-native cyber defense. By uniting Dell’s optimized hardware infrastructure, NVIDIA’s accelerated computing platform, and CrowdStrike’s Falcon cybersecurity architecture, the joint offering delivers comprehensive visibility and runtime protection across the entire AI lifecycle.

The collaboration addresses an urgent operational challenge as enterprises race to implement on-premises and hybrid AI environments. While accelerated computing clusters unlock massive productivity gains and advanced automation, they also introduce complex attack surfaces, including model parameter theft, prompt injection vulnerabilities, unauthorized shadow AI deployments, and supply chain compromise within containerized model registries.

**Securing the Full AI Stack from Silicon to Application**
----------------------------------------------------------

Protecting modern enterprise AI factories requires more than traditional perimeter firewalls or endpoint security tools. The integration embeds CrowdStrike Falcon sensors directly into the compute nodes, model serving layers, and orchestration planes of the Dell AI Factory architecture. This unified approach provides security operations centers (SOCs) with continuous visibility and closed-loop threat remediation across multi-layered AI workloads.

Key operational pillars of the integrated security architecture include:

*   **GPU-Accelerated Workload Protection:** Deploying Falcon sensors across high-performance computing nodes to inspect active processes and block container escape attempts.
    
*   **AI Security Posture Management (AI-SPM):** Continuously discovering shadow AI tools, auditing model registries, and mapping package dependencies across container images.
    
*   **Prompt Attack Detection & Response:** Intercepting malicious prompt injections and data exfiltration attempts targeting foundational language models in real time.
    
*   **Agentic Governance:** Monitoring and governing autonomous AI agent interactions, application programming interfaces (APIs), and third-party tool invocations.
    

"Every enterprise is racing to build AI, but AI without security is an opportunity for the adversary," stated George Kurtz, CEO and founder of CrowdStrike. "Security cannot be an afterthought in an AI-powered world. Dell and NVIDIA are powering the AI revolution, and CrowdStrike is securing it. Together, we're making Falcon's AI-native protection foundational to the AI Factory."

**Simplifying Compliance and Accelerating Production AI**
---------------------------------------------------------

Regulatory compliance and data governance remain critical obstacles for enterprises deploying sensitive AI models within financial services, healthcare, government, and critical infrastructure sectors. The integrated platform helps organizations meet rigorous regulatory frameworks out of the box by generating automated audit trails, maintaining persistent event logging, and enforcing strict data isolation protocols.

Furthermore, because organizations can purchase Dell AI infrastructure bundled with CrowdStrike security through the Dell AI Ecosystem Program, IT and security leaders can bypass fragmented point solutions. This streamlined procurement and deployment model reduces friction, eliminates integration blind spots, and accelerates the timeline from AI experimentation to full production scale.

**Expanding Machine-Speed Defense for the Enterprise**
------------------------------------------------------

As sophisticated adversaries increasingly leverage artificial intelligence to automate cyber attacks and discover zero-day vulnerabilities, security teams must match that velocity with automated, machine-speed defense. The integration between CrowdStrike, Dell, and NVIDIA ensures that threat intelligence gathered across global sensor networks is applied instantly to protect local AI factories within milliseconds of detection.

By establishing a secure foundation for generative AI and agentic workflows, the partnership empowers global enterprises to innovate aggressively while maintaining uncompromising resilience against modern cyber threats.
