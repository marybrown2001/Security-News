Managed infrastructure and cloud services provider 11:11 Systems has officially acquired select IBM VMware Cloud Service Provider (VCSP) enterprise customers. Securing this portfolio through the [11:11 acquires IBM VMware](https://soc-news.com/11-11-acquires-ibm-vmware-enterprise-customers/) customers marks the company’s eighth VMware-related transaction and eleventh strategic acquisition overall. The acquisition significantly expands 11:11 Systems' managed infrastructure footprint across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific, and South America, reinforcing its market position as the world's largest privately held global VMware Cloud Service Provider partner.

The strategic customer transition comes as Broadcom continues streamlining its VMware partner ecosystem, driving major market consolidation toward a select group of high-scale cloud service providers. By absorbing hundreds of enterprise clients hosting critical VMware workloads on IBM Cloud, 11:11 Systems provides a seamless, low-risk operational bridge to modernize, secure, and manage hybrid cloud infrastructure without operational disruption.

**Navigating Broadcom Partner Ecosystem Consolidation**
-------------------------------------------------------

Broadcom’s post-acquisition strategy for VMware centers on consolidating its go-to-market channels around premier service partners capable of delivering global scale, deep technical expertise, and enterprise-grade operational discipline. As legacy partner agreements evolve, organizations running mission-critical VMware environments require stable operational continuity and predictable licensing structures.

Through its established VMware Transition Program, 11:11 Systems offers migrating IBM customers immediate licensing compliance, operational stability, and flexible migration timelines. Enterprise clients can transition workloads to 11:11’s resilient VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF) platform at their own pace, eliminating the need to refactor applications or switch hypervisors during high-stakes cloud migrations.

Key operational benefits delivered through the transition include:

*   **Seamless Workload Continuity:** Transitioning complex enterprise applications from IBM Cloud to 11:11 Systems' infrastructure without mandatory code refactoring or operational downtime.
    
*   **Unified Management Console:** Granting enterprise IT teams single-pane-of-glass visibility across cloud workloads, backup jobs, disaster recovery environments, and storage pools.
    
*   **Predictive Licensing Economics:** Assisting enterprise clients in managing Broadcom licensing transitions through flexible, usage-based consumption models.
    
*   **Integrated Cyber Resilience:** Layering native backup, disaster recovery as a service (DRaaS), and managed secure access service edge (SASE) capabilities directly onto migrated workloads.
    

**Strengthening Global Managed Infrastructure and Cyber Resilience**
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Enterprise organizations operating in highly regulated sectors—such as healthcare, financial services, logistics, and government contracting—face stringent data sovereignty, audit, and disaster recovery requirements. 11:11 Systems builds its cloud regions directly within high-compliance colocation facilities globally, equipping enterprise clients with robust infrastructure resilience and low-latency network connectivity.

Beyond basic cloud hosting, the acquired IBM customer base gains access to 11:11’s comprehensive cyber resilience ecosystem. By integrating continuous data protection, immutability, and automated recovery runbooks into core cloud environments, organizations can recover rapidly from cyberattacks, ransomware incidents, or hardware failures without data loss.

"Broadcom's Cloud Service Provider strategy focuses on partners who possess the technical depth and global infrastructure required to support mission-critical enterprise transformations," stated Ram Velaga, President of Broadcom's Infrastructure Software Group. "11:11 Systems' extensive VMware track record, global footprint, and integrated security portfolio make them exceptionally well-positioned to guide enterprise customers as they modernize workloads on VMware Cloud Foundation."

**Accelerating Workload Modernization for Private AI and Containers**
---------------------------------------------------------------------

As global enterprises evaluate their long-term IT roadmaps, many organizations seek to run traditional virtual machines, containerized microservices, and private artificial intelligence workloads within a unified, secure operating environment. 11:11 Systems' managed platform supports hybrid application architectures, allowing enterprise IT teams to deploy modern Kubernetes clusters and private AI frameworks alongside legacy database environments.

Executive leadership at 11:11 Systems emphasized that acquiring the IBM customer portfolio reinforces the company's commitment to serving as an augmented infrastructure partner for global enterprise clients. By handling complex cloud migrations, continuous security operations, and compliance management, 11:11 Systems enables enterprise IT teams to shift internal resources away from routine data center operations toward core business innovation.

**Expanding Market Leadership Through M&A Execution**
-----------------------------------------------------

The IBM customer acquisition builds upon 11:11 Systems' aggressive growth trajectory across the managed infrastructure market. Following previous successful acquisitions—including iland Cloud, Green Cloud Defense, Sungard Availability Services, Unitas Global, Faction, Ntirety, and Digital Sense—11:11 Systems now serves over 6,000 enterprise clients globally.

As enterprise cloud environments become increasingly complex, 11:11 Systems continues to solidify its positioning as an essential infrastructure partner for organizations seeking stability, performance, and long-term security across their VMware ecosystems.
