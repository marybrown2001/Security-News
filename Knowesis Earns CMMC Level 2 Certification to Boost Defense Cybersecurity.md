Health and defense consulting provider Knowesis has officially completed its Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Level 2 assessment. Achieving independent [CMMC Level 2 certification](https://soc-news.com/knowesis-earns-cmmc-level-2-certification/) validates that the company meets strict Department of Defense (DoD) standards for safeguarding Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) across its digital systems. By completing this rigorous evaluation, Knowesis positions itself as an audit-ready partner for federal agencies and prime contractors navigating evolving federal compliance requirements.

The CMMC Level 2 framework requires full implementation of all 110 security requirements across 14 capability families defined in NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2. To achieve certification, organizations must undergo an independent assessment conducted by a Certified Third-Party Assessment Organization (C3PAO). The evaluation covers extensive documentation, including a detailed System Security Plan (SSP), and verifies that operational security controls function effectively in real-world environments.

**Strengthening Federal Contracting Standards Ahead of DoD Program Shifts**
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

The successful assessment arrives during a transition period for federal cybersecurity enforcement. While the Department of Defense continues refining its CMMC rollout timelines, federal authorities strongly encourage defense industrial base (DIB) contractors to proactively validate their security postures. Completing the Level 2 assessment ahead of mandatory enforcement deadlines demonstrates Knowesis’s capacity to protect sensitive defense data without operational disruption.

In addition to its new CMMC Level 2 status, Knowesis maintains existing international quality and security credentials, including ISO 9001:2015 for Quality Management Systems and ISO 27001:2022 for Information Security Management Systems. These certifications reflect a mature, multi-layered governance framework designed to handle complex scientific, medical, and analytical projects for federal clients.

**Supporting Federal Missions with Validated Security Controls**
----------------------------------------------------------------

As a certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) and Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB), Knowesis provides specialized research, data analytics, and operational consulting to federal agencies. The company regularly supports mission-critical programs across the Department of Defense, Department of Veterans Affairs, Department of Homeland Security, and Department of Health and Human Services.

By completing its C3PAO-led assessment, Knowesis provides prime defense contractors and agency partners with verified assurance that its IT environment adheres to top-tier data protection standards. As federal buyers place greater scrutiny on software supply chains and contractor security, Knowesis’s certified infrastructure ensures seamless collaboration on high-consequence government initiatives.
