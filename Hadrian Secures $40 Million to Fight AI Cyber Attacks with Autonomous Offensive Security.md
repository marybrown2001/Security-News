Cybersecurity startup Hadrian has announced a $40 million funding round to scale its agentic AI offensive security platform and combat the rising wave of automated cyber threats. The investment round was co-led by Forgepoint Capital International and SmartFin, with continued participation from existing investors HV Capital, Motive Partners, Picus Capital, and Oetker Ventures. Bringing the company's total funding to $65 million, the fresh capital will accelerate enterprise market expansion across Europe and the United States while fueling deeper research and development in agentic penetration testing. Securing funding as [Hadrian secures 40m to fight AI cyber attacks](https://soc-news.com/hadrian-secures-40m-to-fight-ai-cyber-attacks/) positions the firm to transform how modern enterprises continuously test and harden their external attack surfaces.

The capital injection comes as malicious actors increasingly deploy generative AI and autonomous scripts to scan networks, discover zero-day vulnerabilities, and launch multi-vector cyber attacks at unprecedented speed. Hadrian’s platform offsets this imbalance by equipping security operations centers (SOCs) with continuous, autonomous threat validation tools that mirror modern adversary techniques.

**Replacing Periodic Audits with Continuous Exposure Management**
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Traditional security auditing relies heavily on annual or quarterly manual penetration tests, which often capture only a static snapshot of an enterprise’s security posture. As dynamic cloud environments, SaaS integrations, and remote infrastructure expand, unknown digital assets frequently drift outside IT oversight.

Hadrian bridges this visibility gap by combining continuous exposure management with autonomous AI agents. The company’s architecture operates through two core engines:

*   **Atlas Engine:** Continuously maps an organization's digital footprint across multi-cloud networks, identifying exposed assets and verifying potential vulnerabilities in real time.
    
*   **Nova Engine:** Deploys specialized AI agents to execute safe, autonomous penetration testing that validates whether a detected flaw can actually be exploited by attackers.
    

By validating exploitability rather than simply listing potential CVEs, the platform eliminates security noise and false positives, allowing enterprise security teams to focus resources on genuine high-risk exposures.

**Proactive AI Defense Built for Enterprise Scale**
---------------------------------------------------

Founded in 2021 by Rogier Fischer, Olivier Beg, and Maurice Clin, Hadrian was built around the principle that effective cyber defense requires thinking and operating like an active attacker. As threat actors adopt automated tools to exploit software vulnerabilities within hours of disclosure, defensive tools must operate at identical speed and scale.

"Everyone can see that AI is fundamentally altering the global threat landscape," stated Rogier Fischer, CEO of Hadrian. "While AI holds immense defensive potential, many security tools focus on automating static workflows rather than active threat emulation. Our platform emulates hacker attack paths to help organizations prioritize risks that present real exposure."

The platform's capability to deliver high-fidelity threat context has attracted major global enterprises across various sectors, including McKesson, NBCUniversal, TotalEnergies, Amadeus, and Damen Shipyards. Organizations utilizing Hadrian’s platform report up to 10x greater visibility into critical attack vectors and an 80% reduction in time to remediation compared to legacy security scanning tools.

**The Strategic Role of Agentic AI in Modern Security Operations**
------------------------------------------------------------------

As modern enterprise infrastructure becomes increasingly complex, security teams face a widening gap between the number of known vulnerabilities and the bandwidth required to fix them. Automated vulnerability scanners frequently generate thousands of alerts, overwhelming analysts with non-critical data.

By utilizing autonomous AI agents to test attack vectors continuously, security operations can move from reactive patching schedules to proactive risk reduction. Emulating adversarial behavior dynamically allows security operations teams to close operational gaps before threat actors can exploit them, ensuring digital resilience across dynamic corporate networks.
