Cybersecurity research firm Frost & Sullivan has named NSFOCUS the recipient of the 2026 Global Anti-DDoS Protection Market Leadership Award. Recognizing excellence in product innovation, technological deployment, and global customer adoption, the award highlights NSFOCUS’s continuous advancements in [anti-DDoS protection](https://soc-news.com/nsfocus-wins-2026-global-anti-ddos-protection-award/) across enterprise and service provider networks. By combining on-premise hardware appliances with high-capacity cloud scrubbing centers, the cybersecurity vendor provides an integrated defense model capable of countering complex, high-volume distributed denial-of-service attacks.

As modern threat actors increasingly execute multi-vector attacks—combining volumetric floods with application-layer exploits and encrypted traffic manipulation—traditional perimeter defenses often struggle to keep networks online. NSFOCUS addresses these evolving vectors through a hybrid architecture that automatically offloads large-scale traffic to cloud scrubbing centers while locally mitigating subtle application-layer threats in real time.

**Advanced Threat Intelligence and Autonomous Mitigation**
----------------------------------------------------------

A core driver behind the market leadership recognition is NSFOCUS's proprietary threat intelligence ecosystem. Powered by continuous machine learning models and global threat sensors, the platform detects anomalous traffic patterns and automatically generates mitigation rules in seconds. This dynamic response engine reduces false positives, ensuring legitimate business traffic reaches internal applications without latency or operational disruption.

Furthermore, the solution caters to large-scale enterprise environments, telecommunications carriers, and data center operators requiring high availability. By offering automated diversion and reinjection capabilities, network administrators maintain full control over traffic management while delegating complex attack mitigation to intelligent security engines.

**Global Footprint and Strategic Cloud Expansion**
--------------------------------------------------

The recognition reflects NSFOCUS’s expanding global infrastructure and long-term investment in cloud-clearing facilities across major internet hubs worldwide. Operating a dense network of scrubbing centers, the company provides low-latency traffic redirection for global organizations operating across strict compliance and data sovereignty environments.

As digital transformation expands the attack surface for financial institutions, government agencies, and critical infrastructure, maintaining resilient network availability remains a paramount security objective. NSFOCUS's award-winning anti-DDoS architecture delivers the scale, precision, and automation required to protect modern enterprise ecosystems against disruptive cyber threats.
